interaction, or usability — no major new business logic).

[Brief plain-English summary of the UI/UX issue.]

[What the user sees or experiences today.]

[Why this is confusing, frustrating, misleading, inconsistent, or difficult —
from the user's perspective, not "the UX is bad."]

## Desired Experience

[What should be clearer, easier, or visually corrected.]

## Copy / Text Changes

[Exact before/after text, if applicable.]

## Evidence

[Urgent / High / Medium / Low]
